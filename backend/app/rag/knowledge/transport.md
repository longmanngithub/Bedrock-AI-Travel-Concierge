# Getting Around a City

Order of preference for most city trips, cheapest first: walking for anything
under ~2 km, then the metro/subway or trams, then buses, then ride-hailing or
taxis for late nights, luggage, or awkward connections.

Tips that apply almost everywhere:
- Buy a rechargeable transit card or day/multi-day pass on arrival; it is
  usually cheaper and faster than single tickets.
- Group each day's activities by neighbourhood so you are not criss-crossing
  the city and wasting hours and fares.
- From the airport, a train or official airport bus is normally far cheaper than
  a taxi and often faster in traffic.
- Confirm taxis use a meter or agree the fare beforehand; prefer official ranks
  or a reputable ride-hailing app.
- Keep a screenshot of your accommodation's address in the local language.

Allow buffer time around opening hours: many museums close one day a week
(often Monday or Tuesday) and last entry can be 30-60 minutes before closing.
