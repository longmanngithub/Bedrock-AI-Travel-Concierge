# Designing a Good Daily Itinerary

A feasible itinerary respects human energy and geography:
- Plan 2-3 major activities per day, not 6. Over-packed days lead to rushed,
  unenjoyable visits and missed reservations.
- Cluster attractions by location each day to minimise travel time.
- Alternate intensity: follow a demanding day (long hikes, many museums) with a
  lighter one.
- Front-load outdoor and weather-dependent plans to the days with the best
  forecast; keep indoor options (museums, galleries, food halls) as rainy-day
  backups.
- Leave the first afternoon lighter to absorb jet lag and get oriented.
- Book time-slot attractions and popular restaurants in advance, and note their
  opening days — many close one day a week.
- Build in meal times and at least one unscheduled block per day for rest or
  spontaneous discoveries.

Each day should have a clear theme or anchor (e.g. "old town + museums",
"coast + seafood dinner") so the plan feels intentional rather than a random
list of sights.
