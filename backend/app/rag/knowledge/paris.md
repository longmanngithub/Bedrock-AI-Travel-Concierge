# Paris, France — Quick Facts for Trip Planning

Getting around: the Metro is dense and cheap; a carnet of tickets or a Navigo
day pass covers most needs, and much of central Paris is walkable along the
Seine. Watch bags on crowded lines (line 1) and around major sights.

Neighbourhood anchors for grouping days:
- Louvre & Tuileries & Île de la Cité: the Louvre, Notre-Dame exterior, Sainte-
  Chapelle.
- Eiffel Tower & Champ de Mars & Trocadéro: best tower views from Trocadéro.
- Montmartre: Sacré-Cœur, artists' square, village lanes.
- Marais: medieval streets, museums, falafel and cafés.
- Latin Quarter & Saint-Germain: bookshops, the Panthéon, Luxembourg Gardens.

Food: bakeries for breakfast and picnic lunches, bistros for classic dishes,
and museum/lunch prix-fixe menus for value. Many museums are free on the first
Sunday of the month but very busy; several close on Mondays or Tuesdays.

Budget notes: sit-down café service near landmarks is pricey; standing at the
bar or picnicking by the Seine is far cheaper. Seasons: spring and early autumn
are mild and pleasant; summer is warm and crowded; winter is cool and grey but
quieter.
