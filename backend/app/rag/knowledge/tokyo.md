# Tokyo, Japan — Quick Facts for Trip Planning

Getting around: Tokyo's rail and metro network is world-class. Buy a Suica or
PASMO IC card and tap through gates; it also works in convenience stores. Avoid
rush hour (roughly 07:30-09:30) on major lines if you can.

Neighbourhood anchors for grouping days:
- Asakusa & Ueno: Senso-ji temple, Nakamise street, Ueno Park and museums.
- Shibuya & Harajuku: the scramble crossing, Meiji Shrine, Takeshita street.
- Shinjuku: skyscraper views (free observation deck at the Metropolitan
  Government Building), Golden Gai nightlife, Shinjuku Gyoen garden.
- Akihabara: electronics and anime culture.
- Tsukiji Outer Market: breakfast sushi and street food.

Food: ramen, sushi, tempura, izakaya small plates and conveyor-belt sushi span
all budgets. Lunch sets are excellent value. Tipping is not customary.

Budget notes: accommodation and set meals can be reasonable, but bar tabs and
taxis add up. A day trip to Nikko, Kamakura or Hakone is a popular add-on.
Seasons: cherry blossom (late March-early April) and autumn colour (November)
are peak; summers are hot and humid; winters are cool and dry.
