# Bangkok, Thailand — Quick Facts for Trip Planning

Getting around: the BTS Skytrain and MRT metro beat road traffic, which is
heavy. River express boats along the Chao Phraya connect many temples. Use
metered taxis or ride-hailing apps; agree tuk-tuk fares beforehand.

Anchors for grouping days:
- Rattanakosin (old town): Grand Palace, Wat Pho (reclining Buddha), Wat Arun
  across the river. Dress modestly — covered shoulders and knees — for temples.
- Riverside & Chinatown (Yaowarat): street-food heaven after dark.
- Sukhumvit: modern malls, rooftop bars, international dining.
- Chatuchak Weekend Market: enormous market, best in the morning before the
  heat.

Food: some of the best-value street food anywhere — pad thai, boat noodles,
mango sticky rice, curries. Eat where there are queues of locals.

Budget notes: Bangkok stretches a budget far; even mid-range hotels and spa
treatments are inexpensive. Climate is hot and humid year-round; the cool, dry
season (November-February) is most comfortable, and the monsoon (roughly June-
October) brings short heavy downpours — carry a light rain layer.
