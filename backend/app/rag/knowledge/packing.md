# Packing Principles

Pack for the weather that will actually occur at the destination during the
travel dates, not for the season at home. Check the forecast (or typical
climate for that month) first, then build the list.

Core carry-anywhere items: passport/ID and copies, payment cards and some local
cash, phone plus charger and a power adapter for the destination's sockets, any
prescription medication in its original packaging, and a small first-aid kit.

Layering beats bulk: a light waterproof shell, one insulating mid-layer, and
breathable base layers cover most climates. Comfortable, broken-in walking
shoes matter more than any other single item on sightseeing-heavy trips.

Weather-driven additions:
- Hot/tropical: sun hat, high-SPF sunscreen, insect repellent, refillable water
  bottle, quick-dry clothing.
- Cold: hat, gloves, thermal base layer, warm socks, insulated waterproof shoes.
- Rainy season: compact umbrella, waterproof jacket, dry-bag for electronics.

Respect local dress norms: some religious sites require covered shoulders and
knees, so pack at least one modest outfit.
