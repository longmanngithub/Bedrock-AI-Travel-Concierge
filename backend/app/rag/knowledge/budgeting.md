# Travel Budgeting Principles

A useful rule of thumb for a mid-range international trip is to split the daily
budget roughly as: 35-40% accommodation, 25-30% food, 20% activities and
sightseeing, and 10-15% local transport, leaving ~10% as a miscellaneous buffer
for tips, SIM cards, laundry and small surprises.

Ways to lower costs without ruining a trip:
- Travel in shoulder season (just before or after peak) for cheaper flights and
  hotels and thinner crowds.
- Stay slightly outside the historic centre and use public transport in.
- Eat where locals eat: markets, food courts and lunch set-menus are far
  cheaper than dinner in tourist districts, often for better food.
- Buy a multi-day transit pass or city attractions pass if you will use it 3+
  times; otherwise pay-as-you-go is cheaper.
- Book intercity trains and popular attractions in advance for early-bird fares.

Always keep a contingency of about 10% of the total budget. If a trip estimate
exceeds the stated budget, the first levers to pull are accommodation tier and
trip length, then paid activities.
