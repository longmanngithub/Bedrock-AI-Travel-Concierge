# Traveller Safety and Health

General precautions that reduce almost all common travel problems:
- Keep digital and paper copies of your passport, insurance and key bookings,
  stored separately from the originals.
- Carry only the cash and one card you need for the day; leave spares in the
  accommodation safe.
- Be alert for pickpockets in crowded tourist areas, transit hubs and markets;
  use a zipped bag worn to the front.
- Buy travel insurance that covers medical care and trip cancellation; keep the
  emergency number saved offline.
- Note the local emergency number and the location of the nearest hospital or
  pharmacy to where you are staying.

Health basics: check whether tap water is safe to drink; if not, use sealed
bottled or filtered water, including for brushing teeth, and be cautious with
ice and raw foods. Check recommended vaccinations for the destination well
before departure. Stay hydrated and pace activity in extreme heat.

Solo and night-time: share your itinerary with someone at home, favour
well-lit, busy routes at night, and trust your instincts about places and
people.
